How to use your PowerPoint speaker notes as a teleprompter

Published 28 November 2026 · ScreenCue by PeteStudioz.ai

Short answer: PowerPoint has a Teleprompter view only while you record a presentation inside PowerPoint. To read your notes while you present live or share your screen on a call, use Presenter View on a second screen, PowerPoint Live in Microsoft Teams, or a separate teleprompter app such as ScreenCue, which shows your notes over your slides and stays out of screen sharing.

Write notes that read well from a teleprompter

  • One idea per line. Short lines are easier to pick up with a glance than long paragraphs.
  • Cue words for live talks, full sentences for recordings. Reading a full script word for word on a live call sounds read; a few prompts keep you natural.
  • Keep each slide's notes short enough to fit on screen. You shouldn't have to scroll in the middle of a slide.
  • Put the opening line of each slide first. It's what you need the moment the slide changes.
  • Mark pauses and emphasis with a word in capitals or a line on its own, so you see them coming.

Set up ScreenCue as your PowerPoint teleprompter

  1. Install ScreenCue from the Microsoft Store (US$0.99).
  2. Select Open PowerPoint and choose your presentation. ScreenCue lists each slide's speaker notes. Standard .pptx files open without PowerPoint.
  3. Select Open teleprompter. Drag it to the top of your screen, just under your webcam, and make it narrow so your eyes move less.
  4. Set the text size and background opacity so you can read it at a glance.
  5. Turn on Sync & control PowerPoint and start the slideshow. The notes change with each slide, and your clicker and arrow keys keep working.

Questions

Does PowerPoint have a teleprompter mode?

Yes, but only while recording: PowerPoint's Record feature offers a Teleprompter view that shows your notes above the slide while you record. For live presenting, PowerPoint offers Presenter View instead.

Does ScreenCue scroll my notes automatically?

No. ScreenCue shows the notes for the current slide and moves to the next slide's notes when the slide changes. If a slide's notes are longer than the teleprompter, you scroll them yourself, so keep click-through off when you need to scroll.

Can my audience see the teleprompter?

Not on a shared or recorded screen on Windows 10 version 2004 or later and Windows 11. People in the room can see it if they can see your screen, and so can a camera pointed at your screen.
