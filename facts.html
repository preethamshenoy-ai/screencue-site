ScreenCue fact sheet

The key facts about ScreenCue in one place, for reviewers, writers and anyone comparing presenter tools.

Updated 28 November 2026

In one sentence: ScreenCue is a US$0.99 Windows app that shows your PowerPoint speaker notes in a private, always-on-top teleprompter that Windows leaves out of screen sharing and screen recordings.

What it does

  • Reads PowerPoint speaker notes on your PC, slide by slide. Standard .pptx files don't need PowerPoint installed.
  • Shows them in an always-on-top teleprompter you can move and resize, with adjustable text size and background opacity, and an optional click-through mode.
  • Keeps the teleprompter out of screen sharing and screen recordings, using Windows' exclude-from-capture setting.
  • With Sync & control PowerPoint on, follows the slideshow running in PowerPoint for Windows, and can move it forward and back. Clickers, arrow keys and Page Up/Down keep working because the teleprompter never takes keyboard focus.
  • Opens Word (.docx), text (.txt) and Markdown (.md) files as a single scrolling script.
  • Loads notes from legacy .ppt, protected or damaged presentations through PowerPoint, when PowerPoint has opened them.

Files it opens

  • On its own: .pptx, .pptm, .ppsx, .ppsm, .potx, .docx, .txt, .md
  • Through PowerPoint for Windows: .ppt, .pps, .pot, and protected or repaired presentations

Privacy

ScreenCue works on your PC. It has no account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics or telemetry, and it doesn't upload your presentations or notes. Read the privacy policy.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't scroll text automatically. You scroll long notes yourself.
  • It doesn't write, rewrite or summarize your notes, and it uses no AI service. It shows the notes you wrote.
  • There is no Mac, mobile or web version.
  • Slideshow sync and control work with the PowerPoint desktop app for Windows, not PowerPoint for the web, Keynote or Google Slides.
  • It can't hide from a camera pointed at your screen or from a hardware capture device; on Windows 10 versions before 2004 a black box may appear in captures instead.
  • On Windows in S mode, the features that work with PowerPoint (slideshow sync and control, Load from PowerPoint) don't work, because they use PowerShell, which S mode blocks.
