ScreenCue privacy policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Summary

ScreenCue is made by PeteStudioz.ai. It is designed to operate locally on your Windows device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share your personal information, speaker notes, presentation content, or usage analytics.

Information processed on your device

When you select a PowerPoint, Word, text, or Markdown file, ScreenCue reads the speaker notes or text from that file only to display it in the app and teleprompter overlay. ScreenCue stores your display preferences—such as text size, opacity, overlay position, and whether PowerPoint sync is enabled—in its local app settings. It does not upload these settings or the selected document content.

PowerPoint integration

If you enable PowerPoint sync, ScreenCue communicates with the PowerPoint desktop application already running on your device. It can read the active slideshow position, read speaker notes from that local presentation when you request it, and send slideshow navigation commands that you initiate. This communication stays on your device.

Network use

ScreenCue has no account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or in-app network service. The app does not intentionally send your notes or presentation information to a server.

Retention and control

Your selected notes remain in the source file you chose. You can remove ScreenCue's local preferences by uninstalling the app and selecting the option to remove app data when Windows offers it, or by deleting its local app data through Windows.

Children

ScreenCue does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. For support or privacy questions, email pete.studioz.ai@gmail.com.
